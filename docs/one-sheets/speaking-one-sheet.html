Dan Mercede

Operator first, then builder. AI workflows with clear ownership, from the first working version to the checks that keep them running.

Bio

Dan Mercede spent four years as a General Manager with full P&L ownership, and fifteen years before that in sales and financial operations for Apple (as a field sales rep with Mosaic Sales Solutions), Diageo, and Nestlé USA. Running a business showed him where automation breaks: nobody owns the workflow, so nobody notices when it drifts.

He now builds AI systems around that problem. He operates a private AI control plane that has run continuously for months on a self-managed cluster, and publishes the reusable parts as open-source tools, including failclosed, schemafit, and mcp-context-budget.
