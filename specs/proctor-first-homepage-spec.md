# Proctor-first homepage

The homepage introduces Dan as an operator and systems builder and gives visitors an immediate project they can inspect. The personal site routes commercial implementation inquiries to Orion Intelligence Agency.

1. A visitor evaluating Dan sees his portrait, established identity, a link to his operating background, and actions for work and direct contact. Selected Work immediately follows. Proctor leads with its working prototype status, its project page and the organizer's finalist credit. failclosed and the production knowledge graph follow with their own labels and links. The first project title is in view on a 390 × 844 phone and a 1440 × 960 desktop. The project link is in view on the phone at default zoom. Microsite links use document navigation.
2. A visitor can go from Selected Work to the gate demo or diagram gallery. Proof introduces the simulator as an interactive example after checkable evidence. All six tool and run combinations retain their comparison, receipt and artifact link. The diagram gallery carries the three former homepage previews and their notes. Existing home bookmarks for Selected Work and the operating background resolve on Home. JavaScript sends older simulator and diagram bookmarks to the current pages. The initial homepage HTML also offers direct links at those fragment targets.
3. A reader can pick the latest field guide or essay, or choose either archive. From the speaking link an organizer reaches the three topics, invitation email, one-sheet, upcoming workshop and past talk. Home shows the current workshop line and one attributed implementation link. Existing public URLs, feeds, identity metadata and the two illustrative reference architectures remain available.
