# Feature Spec: DanMercede.com v2.0 Flagship Reinvention (The Meridian Monograph)

## 1. Outcome and Domain Scope
Deliver the complete architectural and creative reinvention of `https://www.danmercede.com` under Concept I ("The Meridian Monograph") on an independent versioned release branch (`release/danmercede-v2.0`), without modifying or overwriting `main` or losing any SEO equity, URL parity, factual identity claims, or conversion paths.

## 2. Preserved Contracts and Non-Negotiable Boundaries
- **Authoritative Palette and Contrast:** Preserve Midnight Navy (`#020B29`), Deep Navy (`#07143A`), Copper Light (`#FFC08B`), site intermediate (`#F0975A`), Copper Mid (`#E68249`), Copper Dark (`#B95122`), and White (`#FFFFFF`). Labels over solid copper fills always use Midnight Navy (`#020B29`). Zero `text-slate-500`. Zero Unicode long dashes (`U+2013`, `U+2014`, `U+2015`).
- **Editorial Typography:** Self-host `@fontsource-variable/newsreader`, `@fontsource-variable/ibm-plex-sans`, and `@fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono` with metric-adjusted fallbacks and zero render-blocking third-party Google Font links.
- **Deterministic Static Prerender (SSR):** Every route renders deterministically to static HTML (`entry-server.tsx`) with zero `opacity: 0` SSR traps and zero external CDN `<script>` tags.
- **URL, Schema, and Funnel Preservation:**
  - Preserve 100% of sitemap URLs (140 URLs), `/works/<slug>/` rewrites, redirects (`/contact`, `/terms`, `/resources`, `/readiness-scan`), feeds (`/feed.xml`, `/atom.xml`), `/llms.txt`, and Schema.org `@graph` entities (`#person`, `#website`, `ProfilePage`, `CollectionPage`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `ImageObject`).
  - Keep `/about` strictly identity-only (zero CTAs or promotional needles) and factual (`24 Hour Fitness in Escondido`, `Apple as a field sales rep with Mosaic Sales Solutions`, `From April to July 2026 I also worked as a contract architect`).
  - Keep homepage `<main>` free of `"Orion Apex Capital"` and free of modal/pill/download controls that break homepage restraint contracts, while preserving direct links to Proctor, failclosed, knowledge graph proof, gate simulator, diagrams, latest guide/essay, OpsWorld 2027 speaking, and OIA readiness scan.
- **Accessibility and Mobile Touch Standard:** Zero serious or critical `@axe-core/playwright` violations across all routes (`0` `label-content-name-mismatch`, `0` `nested-interactive`, single `<header>` landmark per page), explicit `width` and `height` on all images, `0px` horizontal overflow from `320px` through `1920px`, and `>= 44px` interactive tap targets on mobile controls.
- **Preview Indexing Isolation:** All preview deployments under `*.vercel.app` emit `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`; production `www.danmercede.com` remains indexable (`index, follow`).
